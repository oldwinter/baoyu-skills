# Title Formulas Reference

8 种 hook formulas + straightforward style，用于平衡 title generation。

## Hook Formulas

| # | Formula | 特征 | Example |
|---|---------|----------------|---------|
| 1 | Subversive | 否定 common belief，制造 cognitive conflict | "All de-AI-flavor prompts are wrong" |
| 2 | Solution | 直接给答案，承诺 concrete value | "One recipe to make AI write in your voice" |
| 3 | Suspense | 透露一半，激发 curiosity gap | "It took me six months to find how to remove AI flavor" |
| 4 | Concrete Number | 使用具体数字增强 credibility 和 impact | "150 lines of docs taught AI my writing style" |
| 5 | Contrast | 小原因 → 大结果，或 expectation vs reality | "One doc replaced three months of AI tuning" |
| 6 | Result First | 用 surprising outcome 开头，吸引读者了解原因 | "After using this method, nobody could tell it was AI" |
| 7 | Rhetorical Question | 提出制造未完成感的问题 | "Why can people spot your AI writing at a glance?" |
| 8 | Empathy | 触碰 pain points，触发 shared frustration 或 relief | "Three months fighting AI flavor — I finally broke free" |

### When to pick each formula

| Formula | 适合 |
|---------|----------|
| Subversive | 挑战 mainstream advice 或 debunk myths 的文章 |
| Solution | How-to guides、tutorials、actionable advice pieces |
| Suspense | Personal stories、case studies、journey narratives |
| Concrete Number | Data-driven articles、benchmarks、step-by-step guides |
| Contrast | Before/after stories、unexpected discoveries、comparisons |
| Result First | Success stories、transformation pieces、"I tried X" articles |
| Rhetorical Question | Problem-awareness pieces、diagnostic/explainer content |
| Empathy | Struggle narratives、community pain points、relatable experiences |

## Straightforward Style

不是每个 title 都需要 hook。Straightforward titles 很适合作为替代：

- **Descriptive**：清楚说明 topic 和 scope
- **Declarative**：直接陈述 main conclusion 或 thesis

它们提供 balance；偏好 clarity 胜过 curiosity 的读者会喜欢。

## Title Principles

- **前 5 个字符内出现 Hook**：创造 information gap 或 cognitive conflict
- **Specific > abstract**："150 lines" 胜过 "a document"
- **Negation > affirmation**："you're doing it wrong" 胜过 "the right way"
- **Conversational**：像和朋友聊天，而不是 academic paper
- **最多约 30 字符**：更长 titles 会在 feeds 中被截断
- **Accurate, not clickbait**：文章必须兑现 title 承诺；titles 可以 bold，但内容必须支撑它

## Prohibited Patterns

- 模糊 academic-style："On XX"、"Thoughts on XX"、"Exploration and Practice of XX"
- 纯 shock bait："Shocking!"、"10,000-word essay"、"Must bookmark"
- 没有方向的问题："Where is the future of AI writing?"
